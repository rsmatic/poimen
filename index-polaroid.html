Who we are

POIMEN is a group of pastors who make music together. The name comes from the Greek word the New Testament uses for shepherd, the same word translated “pastor” in Ephesians 4:11.

We serve different congregations during the week and come together to lead worship, play gatherings, and encourage the church through song.

“I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep.”John 10:11
